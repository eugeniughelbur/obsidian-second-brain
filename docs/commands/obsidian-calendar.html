/obsidian-calendar

One calendar command with four modes - agenda (read a snapshot), reconcile (vault vs calendar gaps), meeting (event to note), schedule (task to event)

Type the command, or just say what you want. The skill matches on meaning, so the phrases below are examples of the shape, not a list you have to memorise.
