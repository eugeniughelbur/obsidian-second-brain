/obsidian-brainstorm

Multi-turn Socratic brainstorm - one question per turn until the idea converges, then a design note with named alternatives and one recommendation

Type the command, or just say what you want. The skill matches on meaning, so the phrases below are examples of the shape, not a list you have to memorise.

  • brainstorm with me
  • think this through with me
  • help me design this
  • interview me about this idea
