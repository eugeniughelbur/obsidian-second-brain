/obsidian-ingest

Ingest a source into the vault - the vault rewrites itself around new knowledge. Every ingest updates entities, rewrites stale claims, synthesizes new concepts, and resolves contradictions.

Type the command, or just say what you want. The skill matches on meaning, so the phrases below are examples of the shape, not a list you have to memorise.

  • ingest this source
  • add this article
  • import this
  • absorb this
